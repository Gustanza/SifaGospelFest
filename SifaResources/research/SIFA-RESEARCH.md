## 1. Identity

| Item | Value | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Name | **Sifa Gospel Fest** (also "Sifa Gospel Festival"); "Sifa" is Swahili for *praise* | ✅ |
| What it is | A free, open-air **"Ibada kubwa ya kusifu na kuabudu"**: a large praise-and-worship service built around gospel music, touring Tanzanian regions | ✅ |
| Country | Tanzania | ✅ |
| Slogan / kauli mbiu | **"Kila mwenye pumzi na amsifu Bwana"** ("Let everything that has breath praise the Lord", Psalm 150:6). The 2026 logo lockup shortens it to "Kila mwenye pumzi amsifu Bwana" | ✅ |
| Hashtags | #SifaGospelFest2026 · #SifaGospelFestival2026 · #KilaMwenyePumziNaAmsifuBwana · #SifaGospelFestArusha | ✅ |
| Format | Free entry, no tickets. Runs roughly 12:00 noon to 10:00 pm. Food and drink vendors allowed, but "commerce is not the goal" | ✅ |
| Message | Peace, hope, salvation and healing (amani, matumaini, wokovu, uponyaji) | ✅ |
| Mission (organiser's words) | Gospel music has long lacked big stages that give it value the way secular concerts have. Sifa builds that stage, gives **new and upcoming gospel singers** a platform next to established stars, and reaches young people | ✅ |
| First edition | **2026 appears to be the first edition.** Nothing was found for 2023–2025, and the youth clinic was described as running "for the first time" | 🟡 |
| Other "Sifa Fest"s (NOT this event) | @sifafestke (Kenya) and "Sifa Fest Mombasa" (Deliverance Church Vikwatani, 2024). Unrelated, so don't mix them up | ✅ |

**Quotes usable on the site** (Suma Mwaitenda, Arusha press conference, Oct 2026; translated):
- "We have seen big concerts of secular music, but for a long time gospel music has lacked a big stage that gives it value. We believe God has given us grace to create this platform."
- "There is no entrance fee. We sell nothing but the Word of God, which is free to believers and non-believers. It is a service of praise and worship."
- "We believe that in singing people are set free, people believe again, people are healed, and if they had wounds they become new people."
- (Mwanza) "We believe that in praise there is healing, and many people have continued to be healed through this service."

## 10. Brand observations (from the logo and promo art)

- **Logo colours** (sampled from the master PNG): royal blue **≈ #3854A4** and orange **≈ #F06C20**, on white.
- Logo mark: worshipper with raised hands, a music note, and blue and orange flame ribbons; the "O" in GOSPEL is a flame.
  Bold geometric sans wordmark (Montserrat/Poppins-like, extra bold).
- Event visuals use **deep royal blue stages and screens, orange accents, white**. Promo art leans on gold 3D city names,
  concert light flares and big uniformed choirs.
- That palette lines up with our GenesisExpo pattern study if we map pink → orange and purple → blue
  (see [docs/design/genesisexpo-patterns.md](../../docs/design/genesisexpo-patterns.md)).
