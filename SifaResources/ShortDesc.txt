For Sifa Gospel Fest, the website should feel energetic, faith-centred and event-led—not like a corporate site. Since each edition moves by region, the structure should make the *next festival* the main focus while preserving previous editions as a growing archive.

# Sifa Gospel Fest - Recommended Website Information Architecture

## Primary Navigation

*Home | The Festival | This Year’s Event | Artists | Partners | Media | Contact*

## 1. Home

Purpose: Announce the next festival and drive attendance.

•⁠  ⁠Hero banner
  - Next host region/city
  - Date, venue and event theme
  - Worship in action
  - Gospel music performances
•⁠  ⁠Latest news or social-media updates
•⁠  ⁠Partners and sponsors logos
•⁠  ⁠Contact and social links

## 2. The Festival

Purpose: Explain the identity and purpose of Sifa Gospel Fest.

•⁠  ⁠*About Sifa Gospel Fest*
  - Story, purpose and annual vision
•⁠  ⁠*Festival Experience*
  - What attendees can expect
  - Who the event is for
•⁠  ⁠*Past Editions*
  - Previous regions, dates, artists, images and highlights

## 3. This Year’s Event

This should be the most prominent menu item and update every year.

•⁠  ⁠*Event Overview*
  - Theme, date, region/city and venue
•⁠  ⁠*Programme / Schedule*
  - Gates open, worship sessions, artist performance times and closing
•⁠  ⁠*Line-up*
  - Featured gospel artists, worship leaders, hosts and guest speakers (if any)
•⁠  ⁠*Venue & Travel*
  - Map, directions, parking, transport and accommodation guidance (if applicable)
•⁠  ⁠*Event Guidelines*
  - Entry requirements, prohibited items, safety and attendee conduct

## 4. Artists

Purpose: Celebrate the performers and make the festival discoverable through artist audiences.

•⁠  ⁠*This Year’s Line-up*
•⁠  ⁠*Artist Profiles*
  - Biography, photo, music links and performance information
•⁠  ⁠*Past Artists*
  - Archive of performers from previous editions

## 5. Partners

Purpose: Make sponsorship valuable and easy to understand.

•⁠  ⁠*Our Partners & Sponsors*
  - Sponsor logos, grouped by category where applicable
•⁠  ⁠*Become a Partner*
  - Sponsorship opportunities and benefits
•⁠  ⁠*Sponsor Packages*
  - Downloadable sponsorship proposal / media kit
•⁠  ⁠*Partner Enquiry Form*
•⁠  ⁠*Community & Media Partners*

## 6. Media

Purpose: Build anticipation before the event and preserve its impact afterwards.

•⁠  ⁠*Photo Gallery*
  - Filterable by year, region or edition
•⁠  ⁠*Videos*
  - Highlights, performances, interviews and recaps
•⁠  ⁠*News & Announcements*
•⁠  ⁠*Press & Media Resources*
  - Press releases, brand assets and media accreditation request
•⁠  ⁠*Social Wall*
  - Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok updates

## 7. Contact

•⁠  ⁠General enquiries
•⁠  ⁠Artist and programme enquiries
•⁠  ⁠Sponsorship and partnership enquiries
•⁠  ⁠Telephone, email and social media
•⁠  ⁠Contact form

## Footer

•⁠  ⁠Quick links
•⁠  ⁠Next event details
•⁠  ⁠Partner / sponsor link
•⁠  ⁠Contact details
•⁠  ⁠Social-media icons
•⁠  ⁠Newsletter / WhatsApp sign-up
•⁠  ⁠Privacy policy and terms
•⁠  ⁠Copyright
